## English

### Who we are
「鋤大D計分」is a score-keeping app for the card game Big Two (鋤大Dee). The developer will be listed on the App Store under the individual Apple Developer account used to publish the app. We do not operate our own servers for this app.

### No accounts
The app does not require registration or a user account. We do not ask you to create a login, and we do not collect your name, email, or phone number through the app itself.

### Information stored on your device
Player names, scores, session history, and settings (for example, interface region and animation preferences) are stored **only on your device**. This information is not uploaded to us. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it, unless iOS has a backup you control (for example, iCloud device backup). We do not run our own iCloud sync for this data.

### Advertising (Google AdMob)
If you have not purchased “Remove Ads”, the app shows a banner and, after every 5 completed rounds, may show a skippable interstitial when you tap 「入今局」. Advertisements are provided by **Google AdMob**. Google may collect device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, device and usage data, and information about the ads that are shown or tapped, to provide, measure, and personalise ads, and to detect fraud. How long this data is kept and how it is used is described in [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google’s advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

If you purchase Remove Ads, advertising SDKs should not be used to show ads in the app.

### App Tracking Transparency
On iOS we will show Apple’s App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt before using data for cross-app tracking, if required. You can also change this later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. If you decline, you may still see ads, but they should be less personalised.

### Consent (Google UMP)
Where required (for example, under privacy rules that call for consent to certain advertising uses), the app will show Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form so you can accept or manage advertising consent. You can change your mind later through the consent form or your device settings, as applicable.

### In-app purchases
The optional “Remove Ads” product is a one-time in-app purchase processed by **Apple**. We do not receive your credit card or Apple ID password. Apple’s handling of payment information is covered by [Apple’s Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/privacy/). Purchase status (whether ads have been removed) is stored on your device and can be restored with the “Restore Purchases” button via StoreKit.

### Permissions
The app does not need access to your contacts, camera, microphone, or location to keep score. Advertising or tracking prompts are described above.

### Children
The app is a score-keeping tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising may be subject to Apple’s age rating and applicable advertising rules.

### Changes
We may update this policy when the app’s features or legal requirements change. The “Last updated” date at the top will be revised. If a public web version of this policy is published, that page will also be updated.

### Contact
Questions about this policy: `kenny@vcm-inter.com`.

## 繁體中文

### 我哋係邊個
「鋤大D計分」係一款用嚟記錄鋤大Dee（Big Two）分數嘅 app。App Store 上嘅開發者名稱，會用上架時嗰個個人 Apple Developer 帳戶顯示。呢個 app 冇我哋自己嘅伺服器。

### 唔使註冊
呢個 app 唔需要註冊或者開帳戶。我哋唔會喺 app 入面要求你建立登入資料，亦唔會收集你嘅姓名、電郵或者電話號碼。

### 只儲喺你部裝置嘅資料
玩家名稱、分數、場次紀錄同設定（例如地區介面、動畫開關）**只儲喺你部手機**，唔會上傳到我哋度。你刪除 app 之後，呢啲資料會一齊被刪除，除非你自己嘅 iOS 備份（例如 iCloud 裝置備份）保留咗。我哋冇為呢啲資料另外做 iCloud 同步。

### 廣告（Google AdMob）
如果你未購買「移除廣告」，app 會顯示橫額廣告；每完成 5 局，喺你撳「入今局」嗰下可能會顯示一則可略過嘅插頁廣告。廣告由 **Google AdMob** 提供。Google 可能會收集裝置識別碼、IP 地址、大概位置、裝置同使用資料，以及廣告曝光或點擊資料，用嚟提供、衡量同個人化廣告，以及防止欺詐。資料保留同用途請參閱 [Google 私隱政策](https://policies.google.com/privacy) 同 [Google 廣告資訊](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)。

如果你已經購買「移除廣告」，app 就唔應該再經廣告 SDK 顯示廣告。

### App Tracking Transparency
如有需要，喺用資料做跨 app 追蹤之前，我哋會顯示 Apple 嘅 App Tracking Transparency（ATT）提示。你亦可以之後喺 iOS「設定 → 私隱與安全性 → 追蹤」更改。如果你拒絕，仍然可能見到廣告，但個人化程度應該會較低。

### 同意聲明（Google UMP）
喺法例要求取得同意嘅地區，app 會顯示 Google User Messaging Platform（UMP）表格，等你可以接受或者管理廣告相關同意。之後你可以再透過同意表格或者裝置設定更改（視情況而定）。

### 應用程式內購買
可選嘅「移除廣告」係一次性內購，由 **Apple** 處理付款。我哋唔會收到你嘅信用卡資料或者 Apple ID 密碼。Apple 點樣處理付款資料，請參閱 [Apple 私隱政策](https://www.apple.com/privacy/)。是否已移除廣告嘅狀態會儲喺你部裝置，你可以用「恢復購買」掣經 StoreKit 還原。

### 權限
計分功能本身唔需要取用你嘅聯絡人、相機、咪高峰或者位置。廣告或追蹤相關提示見上文。

### 兒童
呢個 app 係計分工具。我哋唔會故意收集兒童嘅個人資料。廣告會受 Apple 年齡分級同相關廣告規則約束。

### 修改
如果 app 功能或者法律要求有變，我哋可能會更新本政策，並修訂文首更新日期。如果之後有公開網頁版本，亦會一併更新。

### 聯絡
有關本政策嘅查詢：`kenny@vcm-inter.com`。
